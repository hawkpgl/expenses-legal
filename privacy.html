Privacy Policy

Expenses Automation is a private tool used by one business, for its own QuickBooks Online company file only.

  • What it accesses: the business's own QuickBooks Online accounting data (expense records), after the owner authorises it.
  • Where data goes: data stays on the owner's computer. It is not collected by us, shared with, sold to, or published to any third party.
  • Credentials: access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer, outside any source code repository, and are never shared.
  • Retention and removal: the owner can disconnect the app at any time (see disconnect), which stops all access, and can delete local data at any time.

This tool has no other users and does not collect personal information from anyone.
